# Barry Harris Module

This is an independent educational implementation inspired by Barry Harris teaching, not official Barry Harris Institute material.

## BH-01 Major Sixth-Diminished
Example C: C6 = C E G A; Ddim7 = D F Ab B; combined collection C D E F G Ab A B. Teach stable sixth family, diminished family, alternating inversions, melody-note membership, and voice motion.

## BH-02 Minor Sixth-Diminished
Cm6 = C Eb G A with Ddim7 = D F Ab B. Same engine, minor context.

## BH-03 Harmonizing Scale Notes
Given melody, classify family, generate valid inversions with melody on top, rank by smoothness, show alternatives.

## BH-04 Borrowing
Start from sixth-family voicing; allow 1/2/3 voices to borrow from neighboring diminished family. Visually identify borrowed voices, replay pure vs borrowed, quantify movement and resolution.

## BH-05 Related Dominants
Expose dominant interpretations associated with a diminished seventh structure; hold common diminished core visually while roots/interpretations change.

## BH-06 Minor-6 / Dominant Relationship
Example Dm6 = D F A B. Over G bass the upper structure forms a G9 sonority. Teach continuity and bass reinterpretation.

## BH-07 Passing Diminished Movement
Connect stable sixth voicings/inversions and neighboring destinations using diminished structures, preserving melody/bass where constrained.

## BH-08 Melody Harmonizer
Input melody, key, major/minor sixth-diminished context. Output family classification, candidate harmonizations, voice paths, alternatives, MIDI playback, notation.

## BH-09 Free Lab
Key, major/minor, voice count, melody lock, bass lock, borrow count, inversion, related-dominant view, voice isolation.

## Feedback principle
Explain movement. Example: “E and G stayed while A moved to Ab and C moved to B, producing two semitone motions into the diminished family.” Do not merely say “correct Barry Harris chord.”
